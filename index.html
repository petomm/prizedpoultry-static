What's Inside

Every Ingredient
Has a Purpose

HenGuard Worm Defense is a carefully crafted blend of nature's most effective herbs and seeds. Each ingredient was chosen for its specific role in supporting poultry health — no fillers, no synthetics, no compromises.

Parasite Defense

Papaya Seeds

Papaya seeds contain papain and carpaine, natural compounds traditionally used to support a healthy digestive tract and discourage intestinal parasites.

Gut Cleanser

Pumpkin Seeds

Rich in cucurbitacin, pumpkin seeds have been used for centuries as a natural remedy to support gut health and help flush out unwanted parasites.

Antimicrobial Herb

Oregano

One of the most potent natural antimicrobials available. Oregano supports a healthy gut microbiome and has been shown to have natural antiparasitic properties.

Antioxidant Herb

Thyme

Thyme is packed with thymol and carvacrol — powerful antioxidants that support immune function and help maintain a healthy respiratory and digestive system.

Immune Booster

Garlic

A cornerstone of natural poultry health. Garlic's allicin content makes it a powerful natural antiparasitic and immune system supporter for chickens.

Digestive Support

Mustard Seeds

Mustard seeds stimulate digestive enzymes and promote healthy gut flora. They help improve nutrient absorption and support overall digestive health in poultry.

Nutritional Boost

Flax Seeds

Rich in omega-3 fatty acids and lignans, flax seeds support immune function, promote healthy feathering, and enhance overall flock vitality and egg quality.

Antioxidant Herb

Sage

Sage is an antioxidant-rich herb that promotes cognitive health and has natural anti-inflammatory effects, supporting overall flock wellness and vitality.
